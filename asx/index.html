How it's measured

  • Events: every price-sensitive ASX announcement from ordinary shares with a market cap up to $2b (today's cap, not the cap on the day). Collected after the close each weekday; Yahoo keeps 60 days of 5-minute prices, so earlier events are kept with their bars from then on.
  • Entry: released before 10:00, after 15:30 or overnight → the 10:15 price next session (after the staggered open). Released during the session → the close of the first full 5-minute bar after release. No trade within 30 minutes → left out.
  • One trade per entry: when a company puts out several announcements before the same open, they share one entry and are counted once.
  • Move at entry is the entry price against the last price before the announcement. Returns are long only, from the entry price.
  • Costs: brokerage both ways on the parcel, the spread in price steps (0.1c under 10c, 0.5c to $2, 1c above), plus slippage. On a 2c stock one price step is 5%, so cheap shares carry big costs.
  • Verdicts split the trades into an older and a recent half by date; an edge only counts if it's positive after costs in both. Prices are from Yahoo (unofficial) and miss the bid/ask, so treat this as research, not a trading signal.
