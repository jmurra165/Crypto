How the signals and rug flags are worked out

Signal score runs from −100 to +100. It adds price momentum over 5 minutes, 1 hour and 6 hours, the share of buys among the last hour's trades, and whether the last hour's volume is above the 6-hour average. A coin already up more than 300% in 24 hours is marked down. A score of +35 or more with low rug risk is a buy signal, −30 or less is a sell signal, and anything with high rug risk or under $10k of liquidity is marked Avoid whatever the momentum.

Rug risk adds up warning signs: very low or draining liquidity, liquidity that is tiny next to market cap, a pair only hours old, a price collapse, buys with almost no sells (a honeypot pattern), volume far above liquidity, no website or socials, and heavy paid promotion on a new pair. When RugCheck responds, its on-chain findings are added: mint or freeze authority, holder concentration, and how much of the liquidity pool is locked. 60 or more is High, 30 to 59 is Elevated.

These are fixed rules applied to public market data. They cannot see insider wallets, coordinated dumps or news, and a low score does not make a coin safe. Treat the output as a screening aid and not as financial advice. Most meme coins go to zero.
